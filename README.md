## SmartCare Pro

An Electronic Health Record (EHR) platform that digitizes clinical workflows — from patient profiles and medical encounters to specialized services like HTS, ART, ANC/PNC, and pharmacy.

**Technology:** `React` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `Redux, RTK Query`

```yaml
features:
  - Patient Profile & Medical History
  - OPD / IPD Medical Encounters
  - HTS, ART, PEP/PrEP & TB Services
  - ANC/PNC, Birth & Death Records
  - Pharmacy, Investigations & Referrals
  - COVID/Covax & Cervical Cancer Screening
```
